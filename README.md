Controls
T "Taunt" - The player points their gun towards the direction they're facing and laughs. The laugh itself is a slightly pitched down (0.9 pitch) version of DIO's laugh from Heritage for the Future.

F "Mercy" - The user fires off a shot, and somehow that clears the banished players table.

Z "Banish Shot" - The staple banisher move, upon hitting a user, it banishes them, it's pretty simple.

X "Go to Sleep" - The user teleports behind the target and knocks them down to the floor, shooting them over and over, unlike what you'd expect, this does not banish.

C "Bullet On Sky" - A simple AoE move, the player fires in the air, similar to Mercy, and shoots off the heads of anyone nearby, this does not banish.

V "Shot" - A lethal, non-banish version of Banish Shot, upon shooting anyone, or any unlocked part, they turn into red glass before disappearing.
